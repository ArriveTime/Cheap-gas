Official Fuel Price Trends

Use authoritative market data separately from individual station listings.

U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)

The EIA publishes official U.S. retail gasoline and diesel price series. These figures are regional or national market indicators and should not be presented as the current pump price at a specific station.

View Official EIA Prices
Price accuracy: GasLocate.com intentionally does not display invented or stale station-level prices. If a licensed real-time station-price API is connected later, the station cards can be upgraded to show verified price, fuel grade, and update time.
